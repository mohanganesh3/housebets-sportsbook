# Teardown — bet365 (bet365.com)

Date researched: 2026-11-09 (real `date`). Public pages only; no login, no money.
Direct fetch: **BLOCKED** — `https://www.bet365.com` failed (curl exit 000;
geo/bot wall). Two official help pages were reachable via search snippets and
are quoted below; the rest is [WEB]/[INF].

Source labels: [HELP] = official help.bet365.com text · [WEB] = article ·
[API] = third-party docs of bet365's own UI labels · [INF] = inference.

## 1. Information architecture

- **Desktop: 4-column layout** — left sports tree · centre live + upcoming ·
  right = bet slip + promotions [WEB: racinginsider.com how-to-bet guide].
- **Left menu is split into 4 sections: `Top Events · Popular · Frequently
  Visited · Full List`** — criticised as over-granular ("is Frequently Visited
  mine or everyone's?") [WEB: davemorales.work/projects/true-sport]. AU example:
  `Popular` = AFL/NRL/NBA, then the full A–Z sport/race list [WEB:
  onlinebettingaus.com].
- **Header:** utility links `Sports · In-Play · Extra · Responsible Gaming ·
  Help`; green bar with logo + `Sports · In-Play · Search · Join(yellow) ·
  Log in`; "Multi Maker" feature under the logo in the left column [WEB:
  onlinebettingaus.com].
- **Home first viewport:** a new-customer banner (Join Now) + racing-focused
  info panel — home **defaults to horse racing** (heritage Stoke bookmaker);
  football needs one more tap [WEB: bettingapps.uk.com; onlinebettingaus.com].
  "Most popular betting market + daily bet boosts" headline the homepage [WEB:
  racingpost.com].
- **App:** bottom tabs `A–Z Sports · My Bets · Banking · Live Now` (US variant);
  sports as scrollable icons at top; collapsible league menus; search icon
  top-right [WEB: goal.com review; drmonojog.com review; racingpost.com].
- **Density:** famously dense + plain — "information-dense and visually plain
  but it is fast and the layout has barely changed in years, which regular
  users treat as a feature" [WEB: sportyfolio.com]. ~38 sports, deepest
  in-play book of UK operators [WEB: sportyfolio.com].

## 2. Bet slip — placement & behaviour

- Desktop: right rail, shares the column with promos [WEB: racinginsider.com].
- App: bet-slip icon **top-right**; opens a panel with selections, stake input,
  confirm [WEB: drmonojog.com].
- Handles complex multiples + Bet Builder without lag at peak [WEB: sportyfolio].
- **Edit Bet** — add/swap/remove selections on an *unsettled* bet — uniquely
  theirs among majors [WEB: sportyfolio.com; gambling-park.com list].
- On placement: **bet confirmation receipt** with bet reference; history via
  `History → Settled/Unsettled → date range → Bet Details` [HELP: placing-a-bet].
- Cash-out availability is **marked on the slip itself** with live values
  computed [WEB: goal.com].

## 3. Odds changes — display & acceptance

- Official: "odds or line changes will need to be accepted before placing the
  bet, **unless** you have enabled the `Allow odds and line changes during bet
  placement` toggle" — Preferences section of the Account menu [HELP:
  bet-placement].
- In-slip: "the change will be shown on your bet slip… either **remove the
  selection** or **accept the new odds** and continue" — i.e. per-leg
  struck-old→new + explicit accept, no silent mutation [HELP: placing-a-bet].
- In-play prices flash **green when they shorten/up and red when they drift**
  per review description ("turn green when they go up and red when they go
  down") [WEB: yesitfirm.com — MEDIUM confidence on direction wording; the
  flash-on-real-delta behaviour itself is well-established repo knowledge].
- Acceptance delay exists in-play ("short bet delays", confirm new prices)
  [WEB: racinginsider live-betting guide] — no visible countdown (that's
  Betfair Exchange's trick, not bet365's).

## 4. Suspension / locked markets

- Official: "Should a market be suspended, **[a symbol] displays where the odds
  were previously located**" — odds are *replaced by a symbol*, instantly, in
  the same frame [HELP: bet-placement]. The gold standard: loud, immediate,
  unbettable; absence of motion IS the signal (repo 29-competitor-motion).
- Markets suspend around goals/penalties/red cards and "at the off" in racing;
  reopen promptly once the incident resolves — a VAR-window review praised
  their reopen speed vs rivals needing a manual refresh [WEB: bettingapps.uk.com].

## 5. Live score + clock

- In-play rows carry a live game clock (class `ippg-Market_GameClock` exists
  in their markup — [API: github amankhoza/bet365-odds-parser]).
- Event page: virtual field/court animation for non-streamed matches +
  integrated small video player; real-time stats overlay [WEB: yesitfirm.com].
- 600k+ streamed events/yr (funded account or bet in last 24 h) [WEB: sportyfolio].

## 6. Market group tabs & counts

- Soccer match-page tabs (verbatim UI labels): **`Popular · Asian Lines ·
  Goals · Half · Corners · Shots`** [API: apify zen-studio/bet365 docs].
- Other sports: NBA `Points · Threes · Assists · Rebounds · Combos · Defence`;
  MLB `Pitcher Props · Batter Props`; NHL `Goalscorers · Shots · Goalie` [API].
- Depth: ~212 markets on a typical soccer event; 15–20 lower league, 40+ big
  match [API: odds-data-api.com; moviodds.com].

## 7. Cash-out

- **Richest suite in the industry:** full + **partial** (slider, in-slip) +
  **Auto Cash Out** (triggers at a pre-set value) [WEB: bettingapps.uk.com;
  goal.com; ohiobetapps matrix: only book with auto CO].
- Placement: directly in the bet slip/open-bet card — "sitting directly within
  the bet slip rather than buried in a submenu"; button shows the exact amount
  [WEB: bettingapps.uk.com; yesitfirm.com].
- CO availability marked on the slip pre-placement [WEB: goal.com]; offered on
  "far more markets than is normal" [WEB: sportyfolio].
- Known failure: CO suspended exactly when wanted (moment-of-truth) — repo
  review-mine [research/16, sourced pattern].

## 8. My bets

- App bottom tab `My Bets`; rows carry mini score modules; open bets show live
  CO value [WEB: goal.com; repo 06 summary]. Order history path documented in
  the iOS flow: Home → session → place bet → receipt → order history → cash out
  [WEB: pageflows.com bet365 flow].
- Sorting/filtering depth on the bets list: UNKNOWN (login-gated).

## 9. Search

- Search icon in the green header bar + app top-right; "typing a team or
  competition name returns relevant results quickly"; favourites remembered and
  surfaced [WEB: onlinebettingaus.com; bettingapps.uk.com; racingpost.com].
- Direct-to-market results: UNKNOWN.

## 10. Empty & loading states

- UNKNOWN for empties (login-gated). Loading: reputation for near-instant
  loads even at NFL Sunday/NBA playoff peaks [WEB: goal.com]; slip "processing"
  hang at peak moments is the recurring complaint [repo research/16, sourced].

## 11. Promo/clutter in first viewport

- **MEDIUM-HIGH:** "their promotions are larger than any other site in this
  study, and utilize a carousel" [WEB: davemorales.work]. Homepage also carries
  daily bet boosts prominently [WEB: racingpost.com]. Still, density is betting
  content, not banner art — reads "functional not fun" (repo consensus).

## Beat-it list (for the table)

Suspension *reasons* (they show a dead symbol, we show `SUSPENDED · Goal`);
visible accept countdown (they have none); empty states with direction;
market settlement tooltips (Kalshi-style, nobody has); personalization they
explicitly lack (can't reorder home); same density discipline + an actual
motion language vs their ~zero transitions.
