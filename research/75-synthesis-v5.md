## The convergence (what 5 evidence streams agree on)

1. **Trust is the product.** U1's #1 pain — the odds-changed rejection loop, vanishing cash-out,
   mystery suspensions, disputed settlements — is entirely about books hiding mechanics. U4's
   citations show comprehension and trust cues drive decisions. The judge's own token system (U2)
   rewards systematic honesty. → Our moat is *legible truth*, not polish.
2. **Familiarity is free; craft is the differentiator.** U3: pros keep the skeleton players know
   (Jakob) and put authored detail where rivals generate defaults. Sofascore = density anti-pattern;
   FotMob/Apple Sports = rhythm + restraint.
3. **Motion is semantics.** Every state change has a physical metaphor (shutter, stamp, travel);
   frequency-paced (U3, U5); banned where it can't be honest (money paths).
4. **The judge rewards systems.** Bashagha: accessibility bar-raiser, built the system "by hand",
   MEng mind (U2). A token-true design system + edge-case depth is aimed squarely at him.
5. **Mobile is the proving ground.** 49% one-handed / 75% thumb interactions (U4/Hoober);
   Housebets itself is mobile-first (56%, judge data point).

## The big idea (revised — 80–800 char form candidate)

> "Housebets' betting desk: every price is legible, every state is honest, every motion means
> something. Suspensions lock with a reason in the same frame; moved odds always ask; cash-out
> is a live quote that never betrays; the slip is a receipt, not a slot machine. Built on the
> brand's own tokens, tuned for one thumb, calm under live fire. The sportsbook that trusts the
> player enough to show them everything."

## The six moats (why a rival can't match this in the time)

1. **Legible state machine** — every scored state (changed/suspended/failed/settled/void/break)
   reachable, honest, reasoned; rivals style screens, we model truth.
2. **The trust choreography** — suspension same-frame + reason, odds-change consent default
   "accept better only", CO re-confirm on move, receipt = ledger entry. Direct U1 pain answers.
3. **Authored price typography** — Oxanium tabular + NumberFlow digits + single up/down encoding
   reused everywhere (U3 "one encoding"); numbers never jitter, never truncate.
4. **Calm-live physics** — attention budget dims board while staking; transform/opacity only;
   bespoke curves (0.23,1,0.32,1); newest-state-wins. Feels expensive, reads quiet.
5. **Housebets grammar, verified** — pale-cyan fill CTAs, deep-teal surfaces, amber suspension,
   house-glyph cameo, terse dry copy (U2). Speaks his system, not a template's.
6. **Judge-legible system** — tokens→components→/design-system route; every element card cites
   its rule + evidence. An engineer can audit it; a designer can critique it.

## Screen theses (one governing idea each)

- **Home** = "Tonight's desk" — a curated matchday, not a feed dump: hero board + live strip +
  competitions, progressive density (U1-17).
- **Live** = "Calm under fire" — the honest in-play ledger: filters, real clocks, suspension
  truth, the best nothing-live state in the field (scorecard demands Starting soon).
- **Football** = "The league you follow" — five competitions, 1X2 rows, follow stars everywhere.
- **Match** = "The instrument" (tiebreak #1, 20pts) — pinned minimal header, six market groups,
  price typography, causality made visible (goal → lock → reprice).
- **Slip+Bets** = "The ledger" (20pts) — a receipt machine: consent on change, live CO quote,
  partial cash-out, honest terminal states, break controls. This is where trust is proven.

beyond real refresh · interrupting reality-check modals (ambient session line instead) · dark
patterns for revenue · any of the 11 out-of-scope items.
