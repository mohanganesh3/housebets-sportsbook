## Per-book signatures

- **bet365** [H] — market-IA king; yellow-on-green classic cells; partial+auto
  cash-out + Edit Bet (nobody else); star-pin favourites; can't reorder home.
- **Pinnacle** [H] — trader slip: "Risk/To Win", per-leg "Max $X,XXX shown +
  set-to-max", Rebet; NO promos, NO cash-out (deliberate); limits transparency IS
  its brand.
- **Betfair Exchange** [H] — back/lay 6-col ladder w/ liquidity under prices;
  order-ticket slip w/ liability + per-outcome P&L; **in-play acceptance delay
  shown as a progress bar — the only book with visible delay**; cash-out =
  auto-hedge calculator.
- **FanDuel** [H] — mini-slip bar with payout preview BEFORE opening
  (`Betslip • N | $stake to win $Y`); SGP gold standard; always-accept toggle;
  partial CO slider.
- **DraftKings** [H] — dark pill cells; player-prop headshots; **user-editable
  quick-stake presets**; 4 stacked nav bars + promo clutter = cautionary Hick fail.
- **Bovada** [M-H] — 3-col spread/total/money rows; Prop Builder; exotics tabs;
  "unbranded house" look = what we must avoid.
- **BetRivers** [M] — Kambi conventions; scrolling winners feed = ambient social
  proof (our F5 ticker analog); iRush progress bar.
- **Hard Rock Bet** [M] — "For You" first-class nav destination; Flex Parlay
  tiered payout ladder; heritage black+gold.
- **theScore Bet** [M] — media-follow → market auto-pin → alerts = the follow→
  surface→remind loop our Following ships; plain-language market names.
- **Kalshi** [M-H] — contract-question titles; `Yes 63¢` = probability IS price;
  **resolution rules block always visible** ("Settles on official result incl.
  extra time") — trust copy zero sportsbooks have; order-book depth.
- **ESPN BET** [M] — deep-linking scores→markets; competent modern conventions,
  no signature invention = an opening.
- **Stake** [M-H] — NO promo carousel on sports home (restraint is identity);
  flat navy + slate cells + hairlines, terminal-clean; dead-delay live bets =
  most-hated; empty states thin = beatable.
- **Shuffle** [M-H] — glossy black+violet; **persistent ▲▼ direction arrows as
  cell ornament**; `+222` count chips; gradient-3D banner art = the handcraft bar;
  promo banner owns first viewport = their weakness.
