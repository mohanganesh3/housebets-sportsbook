# Personas v2 — evidence-bound (U1/U4/U2)

Each: context, device, prior sites, top frustrations (cited), what feels familiar, delight, exit trigger.

## P-A "Reno" — the experienced crypto bettor
Stake/Shuffle regular, in-play on phone, 22-34, fast thumb, crypto-native.
- Frustrations: odds-change rejection loops (U1 #1 verbatim: "5 tries often"), 18-20s acceptance
  delay, mystery suspensions, cash-out vanishing at the valuable moment, hidden stake limits.
- Familiar: right-side slip, live badge, market tabs, decimal odds, bet history tabs.
- Delight: pending state that's honest and fast; CO as live quote he can trust; implied% per price.
- Leaves when: the slip lies once.

## P-B "Ana" — the casual football fan
Bets on big matches only, scared of mistakes, wants to know exactly what she wins.
- Frustrations: can't read odds formats (U4: comprehension ~11% in BIT RCT), surprise stake
  errors, jargon walls, "where did my bet go".
- Familiar: 1X2 rows, team names over markets, clear stake→return.
- Delight: implied probability in plain numbers; fat-finger-proof stake; a receipt she can find.
- Leaves when: one confusing error.

## P-C "Theo" — mobile-first commuter
One hand, train, bad connection, 49% one-handed stat is him (U4/Hoober).
- Frustrations: top-edge CTAs, lost position on return, slips that reset on nav, dead offline
  states, sheets that fight his thumb.
- Familiar: bottom nav, bottom sheets, persistent pill.
- Delight: ticket pill shows his pick; sheet follows his thumb; offline freezes honestly.
- Leaves when: the sheet drops his pick once.

## P-D "Iris" — first-time crypto bettor
Fiat-mental-model, anxious about USDT amounts and irreversibility.
- Frustrations: crypto-only figures without fiat equivalents (U1-19), irreversible-feeling
  confirm, KYC surprises.
- Familiar: currency units she recognizes, step-by-step receipts.
- Delight: available-vs-in-bets balance split; "your balance is untouched" on failure.
- Leaves when: money math feels hidden.

## P-E "Marco" — colour-blind + keyboard user
Deuteranopia; keyboard-and-sr on desktop.
- Frustrations: color-only up/down, unfocusable odds cells, live regions that spam.
- Familiar: roving tabs, aria labels, focus rings.
- Delight: ▲▼ glyph + word + color triple-coding; "Arsenal to win 1.30" read correctly.
- Leaves when: a state exists only as a hue.

built a 45-component system "by hand", wrote the wireframes as the floor not the ceiling (U2).
