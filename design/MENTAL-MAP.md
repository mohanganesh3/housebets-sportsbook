# Mental Map — the picture a player keeps after one pass

The product is a **desk**: the match is in front of you, your ticket is pinned to your right
(your thumb on phone), and the sports live on your left edge.

## The map (must survive one recall test)

- **Top bar** = identity + orientation: Housebets mark, balance, deposit, search (⌘K). Never moves.
- **Left rail (desktop) / sport row (phone)** = where sports live. Home at top, Live with a count.
- **Center** = the board: matches, markets, the match page. Whatever you're betting on is here.
- **Right panel (desktop) / bottom pill→sheet (phone)** = YOUR ticket: slip + open bets. It is the
  same object on both devices — collapses to a pill showing the pick on phone.
- **Footer/dock** = trust layer: support, responsible gambling, about — reachable, never loud.

## Rules the layout must keep so the map holds

1. The ticket NEVER shares space with the board — it has its own persistent zone (panel or pill).
2. Live is always one tap from anywhere (rail entry + phone nav).
3. A pick's journey is spatial: cell → ticket zone → receipt in My bets. Objects travel, P4.
4. Phone = same map folded: rail→sport row, panel→pill+sheet, board fills the screen.
5. Search answers "where is X" — result rows say where they live (Arsenal · Premier League · Match).

## Recall test (qc/mental-map-test.md)
Fresh context does one pass, then must draw the four zones + describe where the ticket lives and
how Live is reached. Pass = all zones + ticket journey correct without reopening the app.
